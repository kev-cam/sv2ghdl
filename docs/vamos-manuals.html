vamos: simulator CLI reference manuals

Manuals collected for building vamos, the drop-in command-line front end (see docs/VAMOS_PLAN.md). Links were checked on 2026-09-27. Vendor manuals are unofficial mirrors: they are older releases and could disappear. A 403 tag means the site blocked a scripted check. It usually works in a browser.
